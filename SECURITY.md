## Reporting a Vulnerability

The ManiLuaHub engineering team takes security and data integrity very seriously. If you discover a vulnerability or potential exploit related to manifest parsing, Lua script generation, or API endpoints:

1. **Do not create a public GitHub issue.**
2. Email your findings directly to **security@maniluahub.com** or **legal@maniluahub.com**.
3. Include detailed steps to reproduce the issue, sample payloads, and the affected App IDs.

### Response Timelines

- **Initial Acknowledgment**: Within 24 hours
- **Triage & Status Update**: Within 48 hours
- **Fix & Public Disclosure**: Coordinated disclosure after remediation

We appreciate your responsible disclosure and contributions to keeping the Steam modding and backup ecosystem safe and transparent.
